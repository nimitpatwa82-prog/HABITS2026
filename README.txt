1. Create a free account at github.com and a new empty repository (name: habits).
2. In the repository, click "Add file" > "Upload files". Drag in EVERYTHING from this folder
   (including the hidden .github folder, app folder and all files), then "Commit changes".
3. Open the "Actions" tab. Wait for "Build APK" to finish (about 5 minutes, green tick).
4. Open the finished run, scroll to "Artifacts", download "habits-apk" (a zip), unzip it.
5. Copy app-debug.apk to your phone and tap it. Allow "install unknown apps" if asked.
6. On first launch allow notifications and "alarms & reminders".
